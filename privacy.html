小骑士乐园隐私政策

适用：当前国际网页版、安卓及 iOS 测试版 · 更新：2026 年 10 月 15 日

运营及版权主体：合肥市朋程共行电子商务有限责任公司。联系邮箱：xuerji@pengchenggongxing.com。儿童请由监护人联系。

我们在当前版本处理什么

课程进度、星星、外观及语言偏好、棋局存档保存在您当前设备的浏览器或应用存储中。离线棋谱与 Stockfish 大师 AI 随程序提供；当前版本无需注册账号，不接入广告或行为分析 SDK。

受邀好友对弈现已启用。创建或加入房间时，服务端保存随机房间号、邀请码、设备生成的随机标识、入房令牌、双方准备状态、棋局走法与结果，以及应用提供的固定短句交流内容；无需注册账号。只有持有完整邀请码的人可以加入，房间最多两人。房间自创建起 24 小时后失效，过期记录会在后续房间操作时清理。服务端还会临时使用请求 IP 进行限流；网络托管和反向代理提供商可能处理连接日志。当前没有陌生人匹配、自由文字聊天或在线反馈收件服务。反馈可由用户导出为文件；只有您或监护人主动发送给我们时，我们才会收到该内容。请勿在反馈或邮件中填写儿童姓名、学校、电话、住址等不必要的个人信息。

网络访问与邮件

访问国际网页版或使用好友对弈时，网页、接口和网络托管服务提供商可能处理连接所需的信息，例如 IP 地址、访问时间和请求记录；这些信息按其服务规则处理。向支持邮箱发送邮件时，我们及邮箱服务提供商会处理发件地址和邮件内容，用于答复与处理问题。请由监护人发送涉及儿童的支持请求。

保存、删除与变更

设备上的进度和本地棋局由用户控制，可通过设备的应用或网站数据管理功能清除；清除后可能无法恢复。清除设备数据不会立即删除服务端的好友房间。需要查询或删除已发送给我们的支持邮件、反馈或仍保存的好友棋局，请由监护人发邮件至 xuerji@pengchenggongxing.com，说明相关邮件、反馈编号或房间号，避免再次提供多余个人信息。

若未来启用账号、在线反馈或其他数据处理，我们会在启用前更新本政策和应用内说明，并重新核对商店隐私资料。本政策仅描述上述当前测试版本。

Little Knight Academy Privacy Policy

Applies to the current international web, Android and iOS test builds · Updated 15 October 2026

Operator and copyright holder: Hefei Pengcheng Gongxing E-commerce Co., Ltd. Contact: xuerji@pengchenggongxing.com. A parent or guardian should contact us for a child.

Data handled in this version

Lesson progress, stars, appearance and language choices, and saved games stay in storage on your current device. Classic games and the Stockfish engine are bundled for offline use. This version requires no account and includes no advertising or behavioral analytics SDK.

Invite-only friend rooms are enabled. To create or join a room, the server stores a random room ID, invitation code, device-generated random identifier, seat tokens, ready states, moves, results, and messages selected from a fixed set of phrases. No account is required. Only someone with the full invitation code can join; a room has at most two players. A room expires 24 hours after creation; expired records are removed during a subsequent room operation. The service also temporarily uses the request IP address for rate limiting; hosting and reverse-proxy providers may process connection logs. There is no public matchmaking, free-text chat, or online feedback inbox. Feedback can be exported as a file; we receive it only if you or a guardian choose to send it to us. Please avoid including a child's name, school, phone number, address, or other unnecessary personal information.

Web access and email

When you visit the international website or use friend play, the web, API and network hosting providers may process connection information, such as IP address, request time and access records, under their own service terms. If you email support, we and our email provider process your address and message to respond and resolve the request. A guardian should send support requests concerning children.

Storage, deletion, and changes

You can clear on-device progress and local games through your device's app or website data controls; deleted progress may not be recoverable. Clearing device data does not immediately delete a friend room on the server. A guardian can request access to or deletion of support emails, feedback or a still-stored friend game at xuerji@pengchenggongxing.com, identifying the relevant message, feedback ID or room ID without supplying extra personal data.

If accounts, online feedback or other processing is introduced, we will update this policy and the in-app notice before enabling it and review the store privacy disclosures. This policy describes only the current test builds above.
