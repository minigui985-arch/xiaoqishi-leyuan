运营及版权主体：合肥市朋程共行电子商务有限责任公司。联系邮箱：bujibuxushi@gmail.com。儿童请由监护人协助使用和联系。

本机使用

课程进度、星星、外观及语言偏好、棋局存档保存在当前设备的浏览器或应用存储中。离线棋谱和 Stockfish 引擎随程序提供。当前版本无需注册账号，不接入广告或行为分析 SDK。

邀请好友对弈

国际网页版的好友房间通过位于美国的 UCloud 主机提供。创建或加入房间时，服务器保存随机房间号、邀请验证信息、席位验证信息、棋盘状态、走棋记录、双方准备状态及用户点击的预设友好短句。邀请码和席位信息也会保存在当前设备，以便刷新后恢复。系统不提供陌生人随机匹配或自由文字聊天；请只向认识的朋友分享邀请，儿童应由监护人协助。

房间创建满 24 小时后不能继续使用；过期记录在后续房间请求时清理，因此服务器上的删除可能晚于 24 小时。服务会在内存中短暂使用连接 IP 地址限制请求频率；网络和托管服务提供商也可能按其服务规则处理 IP、时间及连接记录。好友房间数据不会用于广告或用户画像。

反馈与删除

应用内导出反馈只在本机生成文件，不会自动发给我们。若您主动发邮件，邮箱服务会处理发件地址和内容。请勿在反馈或邮件中提供儿童真实姓名、学校、电话或住址。设备数据可通过浏览器或应用的数据管理功能清除。监护人如需查询或删除支持邮件、反馈或未过期房间数据，请联系上述邮箱，并提供对应邮件或房间号，不要发送席位密钥。

若将来新增账号、收费、广告、公开匹配或其他数据处理，我们会在启用前更新本政策与应用说明。

Little Knight Academy Privacy Policy

International web and mobile test builds · Updated 14 October 2026

Operator and copyright holder: Hefei Pengcheng Gongxing E-commerce Co., Ltd. Contact: bujibuxushi@gmail.com. A parent or guardian should help children use the service and contact us.

On-device use

Lesson progress, stars, appearance and language preferences, and saved games stay in storage on your device. Classic games and Stockfish are bundled for offline use. No account, advertising, or behavioral analytics SDK is required in this version.

Invited friend games

Friend rooms on the international website run on a UCloud host in the United States. When a room is created or joined, the server stores a random room ID, invitation and seat verification data, board position, moves, ready status, and selected preset friendly phrases. The invitation and seat information is also saved on your device so the game can resume after a refresh. There is no public stranger matching or free-text chat. Share invitations only with people you know; a guardian should help a child do so.

A room stops working 24 hours after creation. Expired records are removed on a later room request, so deletion from server storage may occur after 24 hours. The service briefly uses connection IP addresses in memory to limit request frequency. Network and hosting providers may also process IP addresses, timestamps, and connection records under their own terms. Friend-room data is not used for advertising or profiling.

Feedback and deletion

Exporting feedback only creates a file on your device; it is not sent to us automatically. If you email us, the email provider processes the sender address and message. Do not include a child's real name, school, phone number, or home address. You can clear device data through browser or app settings. A guardian can ask about or request deletion of support mail, feedback, or an unexpired room by contacting the address above with the relevant message or room ID. Do not send seat secrets.

We will update this policy and the in-app notice before adding accounts, payments, advertising, public matching, or other data processing.
