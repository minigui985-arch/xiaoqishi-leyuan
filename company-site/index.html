产品与联系

小骑士乐园当前为 v0.8.8 国际网页版公开测试（2026-11-06 更新），提供棋盘、棋子与走法启蒙课程、12 局历史名局回放、Stockfish 离线 AI 练习、十字军与明军双阵营，以及儿童 Q 版和成人版皮肤。核心界面支持十种语言，部分长篇说明以英语显示。

About the company

Hefei Pengcheng Gongxing E-commerce Co., Ltd. develops and operates Little Knight Academy, an educational chess experience themed “Two Kingdoms” (Crusaders × Ming), with lessons, classic games and play. Children should use it with a parent or guardian.

The web beta (v0.8.8, updated 2026-11-06) includes same-device play and friend-room testing; friend rooms require an internet connection. Some devices support adding the app to the home screen, and some content is available offline after it has been cached. 3D is experimental; adult mode currently uses the children's 3D models. Please refer to official announcements for app store releases. For product support, contact xuerji@pengchenggongxing.com.
